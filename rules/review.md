# Review Rules

## Purpose

Make reviews prioritize behavioral risk over formatting.

## Audience

Reviewers and agents performing self-review.

## Update when

Update when review priorities or required evidence change.

## Review order

1. Wrong task ownership or phase transition.
2. Infinite loop, unbounded retry or new automatic stop.
3. Coordinate, stale snapshot or postcondition error.
4. Movement priority or input-release regression.
5. DPB compatibility and packaging impact.
6. State reset and session accounting.
7. Tests, logs and documentation.
