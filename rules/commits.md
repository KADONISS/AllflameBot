# Commit Rules

## Purpose

Keep history searchable and safe for release investigation.

## Audience

Contributors and AI agents preparing commits.

## Update when

Update when repository branching or release policy changes.

## Rules

- Use an imperative, scoped subject such as `Document Voyage recovery contract`.
- Keep unrelated changes out of the commit.
- Do not commit generated DLL/PDB, logs, settings, screenshots or secrets.
- Mention validation and known unavailable checks in the handoff.
- Do not amend existing commits unless explicitly requested.
